About Elite Epoxy Solutions

Who we are

Meet Jacob and his family

Hi, I’m Harlan “Jacob” Rudd, the owner of Elite Epoxy Solutions, LLC.

This is my fiancée, Caeley, and our son, Weston. They are my biggest supporters and the reason I work so hard every day.

We’re a family-owned and operated business based out of Dinwiddie County, Virginia, proudly serving homeowners and businesses throughout the surrounding areas. At Elite Epoxy Solutions, we believe every customer deserves quality craftsmanship, honest communication, and a floor that’s built to last.

Whether it’s a garage, basement, patio, workshop, or commercial space, we take pride in transforming ordinary concrete into something you’ll love every time you walk in.

For me, this business is about more than epoxy floors. It’s about building something my family can be proud of while serving our community with honesty and quality work. Every project is treated as if it were my own, and I’m grateful for every customer who trusts us.

If you’ve been thinking about upgrading your floors, I’d love the opportunity to help. Reach out today for your free estimate!
