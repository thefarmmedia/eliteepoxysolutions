Frequently asked questions

Get quick answers to common floor coating questions before you request a quote.

How much do garage floor coatings cost?

Flake epoxy is estimated at $6–$8 per square foot. Final cost depends on concrete condition, preparation and other project details.

Do you grind concrete before coating?

Mechanical surface preparation is commonly important for coating adhesion. The exact prep method will be confirmed after inspecting the slab and chosen system.

How long does installation take?

Project duration and return-to-use times depend on the floor, product selection and conditions. Confirm the schedule during your estimate.

Can cracks or existing coating be repaired?

Many surfaces can be prepared or repaired, but underlying movement and moisture issues may affect the recommendation. Ask for an evaluation.

What are flake floor colors?

Decorative chip finishes combine multiple colors. Physical samples can help confirm appearance before a final selection.

Are outdoor coatings different?

Yes. Exposure to sunlight, moisture, water and seasonal weather can affect material selection and finish texture.

Do you work on commercial floors?

Commercial floor inquiries are welcome. Share your use case, floor area, timeline and site details to discuss fit.

What service areas do you cover?

The business is based in Dinwiddie County, Virginia. Please contact the team to confirm coverage at your specific address.

Can I get a quote online?

Yes. Send your contact details, location and a description through the contact form to request a follow-up.

Are price calculator results guaranteed?

No. The calculator uses $6–$8 per square foot for flake epoxy as a preliminary estimate. Actual project quotes depend on site conditions.
